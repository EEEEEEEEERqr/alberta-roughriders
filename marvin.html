Our Mascot

Marvin Wobick is the heart of the Alberta Roughriders. We carry his name and spirit as our mascot — hard work, loyalty, and that old-school Alberta grit that never quits.

Every shift, every cheer, every pink aura moment on the ice is a nod to the kind of man Marvin was: steady, tough, and always there for family and community.

In Memory

Marvin Robert Wobick passed away on Wednesday, July 12, 2017 at South Health Campus in Calgary at age 91. He was born in Warner, South Dakota to Robert and Mary (Grohnke) Wobick. The family moved to Barons in 1927 to farm in the district.

Marv was a hard worker and a man of many talents — he drove semi, worked at a tire factory, built his own house, climbed telephone poles in stormy weather, and farmed from before sunrise to after sundown. He married Alice Huff on March 29, 1950. They raised three children: Brenda Lynn, Patricia Dawn, and Kevin Robert. He curled into his eighties and golfed until the year before he passed.

He is remembered by his son Kevin (Donna), grandsons, great-grandchildren including Brady Wobick, his sister Beverly Perry, and many nieces and nephews. He was predeceased by his wife Alice, daughters Brenda and Patty, and grandson Jared.

Full obituary: Lethbridge Funeral Home — Marvin Robert Wobick

Meaning to the Roughriders

We made Marvin our mascot because he represents everything this team stands for: family, grit, and showing up. His great-grandsons and the Wobick name are part of the Roughriders story. When the pink hits the ice, Marvin is with us.

Rest easy, Marvin. The Roughriders ride for you.
