Our Mascot

Marvin Wobick is the heart of the Alberta Roughriders. We carry his name and spirit as our mascot — hard work, loyalty, and that old-school Alberta grit that never quits.

In Memory

Marvin Robert Wobick passed away July 12, 2017 at age 91. Born July 23, 1925 in Warner, South Dakota. Farmer, family man, curler, and the spirit behind this team.

Full obituary: Lethbridge Funeral Home

Meaning to the Roughriders

When the pink hits the ice, Marvin is with us. Rest easy, Marvin. The Roughriders ride for you.
